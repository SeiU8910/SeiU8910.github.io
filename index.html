Hermes Local Context (Personal)

A private assistant operated by its owner on a local Mac. It reads selected Google Drive documents and spreadsheets to support personal context and research through local files and Slack.

The Google integration requests read-only Drive, Docs and Sheets access. It does not upload, modify or delete Google files. Accounts are selected by Slack channel; source snapshots and credentials are stored separately on the owner's Mac.

When requested, selected source contents may be included in requests to the configured language-model provider. Replies and requested files may be sent to the owner's Slack workspace. The owner should select only material appropriate for those services.

This integration is for the owner's personal accounts and is not offered as a public service.
